The Problem

Industrial organizations invest in firewalls, network segmentation, and access controls to separate IT from OT. The assumption is that these boundaries enforce the separation documented in the architecture diagrams. But assumptions are not evidence. VTR verifies what actually happens at the boundary.

What we verify

Do your firewall rules match your architecture? Are there routes that bypass the documented separation? What traffic actually crosses the IT/OT boundary? If a control fails, what is observable and what is silent?

How we work

We observe, document, and classify boundary behavior using a proprietary evidence-based methodology. Every finding is traceable. Every conclusion is limited to what the evidence supports.

Deliverables

  • 01 Boundary MapDocumented inventory of every identified boundary point between IT and OT, including firewalls, VLANs, jump hosts, remote access paths, and management interfaces.
  • 02 Verification ReportFor each boundary: what was expected, what was observed, and whether the separation property is preserved, divergent, or undetermined.
  • 03 Action PlanPrioritized, practical recommendations that your team can implement with existing resources.

What we do not do

  • We do not perform penetration testing
  • We do not sell products or licenses
  • We do not install software on your OT network
  • We do not issue certifications
  • We do not guarantee that your plant is secure

We deliver evidence. What you do with it is your decision.
